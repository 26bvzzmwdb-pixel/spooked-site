Entries close October 31, 2026, at approximately 9:00 PM Eastern Time (ET). The Sponsor may publish the final cutoff on @SpookedHandsSOL if needed.

To enter, during the entry period:

  • Create an original video about panic-selling, paper hands, or being “spooked” by a red candle.
